<br>

A snake and a swan meet in a bar.

Their laugh so shared between them chimes.

One slides forward and one beats back 

in curious sympathetic movement dance. 

<br>

Somewhere, a clock ticks its time, 

and the sun between them makes to rise, 

and knowing there is nothing spare, 

the two part hurried, chaste and jest. 

<br>

A glimmer skant off gilden scale, 

a feather born departed tail, 

a kiss of wind as dawn defines 

what lies between the earth and sky.
