# My MOTH Poem
Welcome to my world

Drink of all you see

Dip your hand in gold

River flowing East
 
<br>

Welcome to my world

Flap and flap your winds

Sip the twinkling stars

Sing the win song glee

<br>

Part the still hairs on my head

Rise as moonbeam slant my bed

Shed the form of blood and flesh

Rise to greet the dreamt and dead

<br>

In my world

Lap the tree bark sweet

Smooth the jelly earth

Ride the swelling sea

<br>

Look about my world

Free the long-lived soul
