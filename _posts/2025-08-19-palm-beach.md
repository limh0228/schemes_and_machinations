A poetic response to Gov. Hochul, regarding her brave resistance against Mamdani-proposed tax increases on NYC billionaires.

  </figcaption>

<br>

See paradise: a glittering sliver of sunlit sand, shampooed dogs barking to and fro

<br>

the cheap glory of sunset on slick, tan bodies, well to do and strong from sailing inside a chipper Atlantic Ridge.

<br>

In Brooklyn this morning, the garbage truck tinkles by collecting a bike with no wheels. Who would steal just the wheels?

<br>

Who, such hopeful a creature, collects parts for movement, but does not take the saddle?

<br>

What Thesian ship can be mangled together with these mutt parts strong enough to bear eastern winds down from Manhattan to Palm Beach?

<br>

Stop him! Someone stop him!

<br>

Please! *Please someone*

<br>

Stop that man!

<br>

I don’t want to lose more people to –

<br>

*I cannot, I cannot bear to lose more people to –*

<br>

the sensuous sands, the New York City of southern Florida, if you will:
<br>

Palm Beach.
