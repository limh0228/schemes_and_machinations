#### Decision Boundary of One-Class SVM in t-SNE space

The following graphs are made by feeding the dimension-reduced exported spotify data to a one-class SVM to get a sense of what the frontier of my normal taste looks like in that space, [heat-map-of-the-universe-style](https://www.nasa.gov/mission_pages/planck/multimedia/pia16873.html).

<br>

[t-SNE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-distributed_stochastic_neighbor_embedding) is a method for visualizing high-dimensional data in low-dimension. Songs which are more alike will be nearer each other in the feature space, but we can't visualize a space with that many dimensions. What we can do is reconstitute the points in 2D, attempting to preserve the pairwise distances, the notions of similarity, between songs. Read [this](https://medium.com/cuepoint/visualizing-hundreds-of-my-favorite-songs-on-spotify-fe50c94b8af3) for more info!
