#### We go there
#### when the wind is blowing,
#### when the heart is beating.

#### We go there
#### when the chick is molting,
#### when the flock retreating.

#### We go there
#### when the child is crying,
#### when the rest are reading.

#### We carry on and on
#### the atrophy of living.
