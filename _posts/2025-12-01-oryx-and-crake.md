Loved this book. I see why people like that Margaret Atwood lady. 

<br>

This is a post-apocalypse story. The book has a good mix between present action and internal musing and memory replay. It is very much like watching the narrator, Snowman, think through the purpose of existence in real time, or more specifically, why one should live after he has survived. In theme, it reminds me of the Myth of Sisyphus by Albert Camus, but in tone, this book is more fun and flirty.

<br>

The characters are all distinctly unlikable, and therefore interesting and real.

<br>

This is the first of a trilogy, and Reddit says it stands alone, so I will not be finishing the trilogy. But I absolutely ripped through this one.

<br>

10/10 very recommend!
