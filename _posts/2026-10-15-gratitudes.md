### My gratitudes

I am grateful that the weather is turning and how these days there is often a pleasant wind, and I am grateful to accompany the passage of time in relatively good health and fortune and great company, and I am grateful for the ability to endure continuously, and I'm grateful for the variety of dreams and my occasional memory of them and how they allow me to be with the beloved, and I am grateful for the variation of the tides as it keeps things interesting.

<br>

My life. My love, My freedom, And happiness.
